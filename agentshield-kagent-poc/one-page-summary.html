AgentShield: benefits and
security posture

AgentShield could connect our workloads, identities, policies, and supported traffic observations into an evidence-backed security graph. This would help us find risky paths, explain security changes, and prioritize reviewed fixes.

Expected benefits for the PoC. Our integration and risk reduction have not yet been demonstrated.

How it improves our posture

BenefitImprovement for our platform
Clearer visibility and blast radiusJoin workload identity, services, and governing policies to assess supported paths to sensitive systems and expose coverage gaps.
Earlier detection of security driftWith detection enabled, flag relevant policy changes and unexpected observed communication before a manual incident review.
Faster, supported investigationGive kagent findings with resource identities, graph revisions, and evidence references. Reduce manual correlation and make conclusions easier to challenge.
Focused least-privilege changesPropose specific policy changes, then prove reduced reach with denied unauthorized requests and successful legitimate requests.
Controlled, reviewable responseKeep human approval and scoped Argo/Flux delivery. Record the approval identity, reviewed diff, verification, and rollback in our existing process.

How it fits

AgentShieldObservations and findings

The security gain comes from detecting supported risky changes and closing verified paths. Inventory and policy declarations alone do not establish effective enforcement.

What we must prove

Use an isolated Proxmox cluster and dummy services. Detect a new path from a narrow-role workload to a dummy sensitive service, pass the finding to kagent, and prepare a reviewed fix. Test a benign control, block the unauthorized request, preserve required traffic, and prove rollback.

Measure detection time, evidence quality, false alarms, model cost, and time to a reviewable change. Carry the reviewed image/chart, evidence contract, fixtures, and receipts into an AKS lower environment. Configure workplace identity, telemetry, model routing, and GitOps locally.

Current limits

The API needs external authentication/access controls. The default collector can read all Kubernetes Secrets. Agentgateway access-log and Cilium Hubble transports are unwired in the reviewed source. Azure cloud identity/network coverage is absent. Detection needs explicit activation. The adapter and our runtime integration remain proposed.
